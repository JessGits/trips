~10:00 PM Check in · The Wallace
242 W 76th St · between Broadway & West End Ave

Front desk is 24-hour. Check in, get the room keys, drop the bags. The Wallace lobby has a small bar and lounge if anyone wants to peek — live music runs Thursday through Sunday, so tonight it'll be quiet.

Take ten minutes. Bathroom break, splash of water, swap into walking shoes. Don't unpack tonight. Down to the lobby, out the door, four blocks south.

~10:30 PM Gray's Papaya
2090 Broadway at W 72nd · open until 11pm

Open since 1973. Two hot dogs and a papaya drink — the "Recession Special" — runs about $7.50. Cash or card, doesn't matter. There's no seating, just a counter facing the window.

Standard order: two dogs with mustard, sauerkraut, and onions; one papaya drink to share or one each. The banana daiquiri and the piña colada are fruit drinks (non-alcoholic) if anyone wants to branch out, and chili-cheese is on the menu.

🍴 Bourdain

Gray's Papaya and Papaya King are cousins — Nicholas Gray opened Gray's in 1973 after splitting from Papaya King, taking the formula uptown. Anthony Bourdain hit Papaya King for the same combo on The Layover and called the hot-dog-and-tropical-drink pairing one of the city's quintessential rituals. Walking off an airport rideshare and going straight to the papaya stand for two dogs at 10:30pm is the move. He'd approve.
