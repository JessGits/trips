The Trip

Buckle up, we're about to spend nine days exploring iconic New York City for Ant's 21st birthday. The Upper West Side of Manhattan is home for the week: The Wallace is two blocks from Central Park, just off the Hudson River, and around the corner from a subway that will take us anywhere downtown.

There's a reason people fall in love with this city. Tap any day below to see our week of exploring one of the greatest cities in the world.
