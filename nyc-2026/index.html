The Trip

Buckle up, we're about to spend nine days exploring iconic New York City for Ant's 21st birthday. The Upper West Side of Manhattan is home for the week: The Wallace is two blocks from Central Park, just off the Hudson River, and around the corner from a subway that will take us anywhere downtown. Good restaurants two blocks in every direction, the park five minutes east, and the kind of neighborhood you'll want to walk through on the way back at night.

There's a reason people fall in love with this city. Tap any day below to see our week of exploring one of the greatest cities in the world.
