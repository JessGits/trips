The Village Walk

🚶
From Carbone, walk west — Washington Square Park is 3 blocks north on Thompson, then the West Village opens up beyond it · the whole walk is under a mile · plan ~2 hours including stops
~2:45 PM Washington Square Park
5th Ave at Waverly Place · 3 blocks north of Carbone

Stay as long as it calls — the park rewards both a quick pass-through and a longer linger.

Chess tables — southwest corner of the park, near the Garibaldi statue. Permanent stone boards. Peak action 1–9 PM. Hustlers will offer a game for $5 if anyone wants to play.

Live jazz — informal musicians play around the fountain most afternoons in good weather.

Wander the paths, sit under the arch, watch NYU spill across the lawns.

Before Top of the Rock

4:30 — 6:40 PM Anthony's Choice
birthday boy picks the pre-show vibe

Two ways to spend the window before going up. Anthony's call.

Option 1 · Back to The Wallace. Rest, shower, change. Leave the hotel by 6:40 PM and head straight to Rockefeller for the 7:00 entry.

Option 2 · Head straight to the Rockefeller area. Subway down around 5:30, wander 5th Avenue and the Rockefeller plaza before going up. The flagship stores on 5th between 49th and 59th — Saks, Tiffany's, the Apple Cube — are walking distance, and Radio City Music Hall is one block from the entrance. The Tony Awards air the next night, so red carpet setup may already be visible around Radio City.

Either way — it's windy at the top, dress accordingly.
