The Two Tracks

Track 1
The SoHo Wander

Cobblestone streets, cast-iron facades, and one of the densest shopping strips in the country. SoHo is where streetwear culture (Kith, Aimé Leon Dore, Supreme) sits beside designer flagships (Prada, Chanel, Bottega) and indie boutiques. Walk it slow. End with the natural exit north to Forbidden Planet and Strand at Union Square.

🚇
1 train from 72nd to Houston St. ~25 min, no transfers. Or stay on to Canal St if you want to start further south.
Orientation The Lay of the Land
Roughly: Houston St (north) · Canal St (south) · Lafayette (east) · West Broadway (west)

SoHo is six by six blocks. The big-name flagships line Broadway and Spring Street. The boutique-dense, less-crowded streets are Mercer, Greene, Wooster, Crosby, and Prince — the cobblestone heart of the neighborhood. Walk in a loop, not a line.

✨ Hidden gem

Look up. SoHo has the largest concentration of cast-iron architecture in the world — 1860s industrial loft buildings with ornate facades that look like stone but are painted iron. Greene Street between Spring and Broome is the best stretch. The buildings are as much the show as the stores.

Anchor stop Kith
337 Lafayette St · three floors, since 2011

One of the most influential streetwear and sneaker stores in the world. Founder Ronnie Fieg's curated mix of Kith's own collections plus collabs with Adidas, New Balance, Nike, Comme des Garçons, BMW, and others. The interior is its own attraction — three floors of polished concrete and architectural display.

📍Open in Maps
✨ Hidden gem

Kith Treats — the cereal-and-soft-serve bar inside the store. Custom soft-serve creations layered with cereal (Cinnamon Toast Crunch, Fruity Pebbles, etc.) and toppings. A SoHo cult thing. Worth a stop even if you're not buying anything else.

Anchor stop Aimé Leon Dore (ALD)
214 Mulberry St · technically Nolita, 3 blocks east of Kith

NYC's most distinctive contemporary brand — "urban preppy" with deep '90s NYC roots. Founded in Queens, now globally watched. The Mulberry flagship is a destination in itself: leather couches, vintage Jordans on the shelves, basketball-court flooring. Expect a line on weekends; the staff manages it well.

📍Open in Maps
✨ Hidden gem

Café Leon Dore next door (also 214 Mulberry) — Greek-inspired coffee shop attached to the store. The Freddo cappuccino is the signature. Even if you skip the store, the café is a strong coffee stop. Outdoor seating only.

For the sneakerheads Streetwear & Sneaker Pocket
Howard St, Broadway, surrounding blocks

If anyone's chasing sneakers or streetwear specifically:

· Stadium Goods (47 Howard St) — the sneaker resale mecca. Rare Jordans, limited-edition Yeezys, vintage Air Maxes — market prices, not retail.
· Nike SoHo (611 Broadway) — five-floor flagship with a basketball court on the top floor and a treadmill to test running shoes.
· Supreme (190 Bowery) — the OG. East of SoHo proper, about 8 blocks. Lines depend on what's dropped that week.
· Bape (A Bathing Ape) (91 Greene St) — iconic Japanese streetwear, shark hoodies, camo patterns.

If the day calls for it The Broadway Flagships
Mostly along Broadway between Houston and Canal

For the accessible/mainstream flagship walk:

· Apple SoHo (103 Prince St) — set in a former post office, the original glass-staircase store.
· Prada Broadway (575 Broadway) — the Rem Koolhaas-designed flagship, more architecture exhibit than store.
· Uniqlo Global Flagship (546 Broadway) — massive, multi-floor, the most efficient stop for actual closet replenishment.
· Adidas Flagship (115 Spring) and Converse (560 Broadway) for sneaker classics.

If vintage calls What Goes Around Comes Around
351 W Broadway · high-end vintage and luxury resale

The vintage authority — authenticated Chanel, vintage Levi's, archival Versace, classic band tees. Pricey, but the curation is the point. Worth a walk-through even if not buying.

📍Open in Maps
When hungry Lunch in SoHo
No reservations · walk in wherever calls

SoHo has more restaurants than blocks. A few honest options:

· Balthazar (80 Spring) — the French brasserie that defined SoHo dining. The bar is walk-in-friendly even when the dining room is booked.
· Emilio's Ballato (55 E Houston) — old-school red-sauce Italian, longtime celebrity hangout, Bourdain favorite.
· Lombardi's (32 Spring) — America's first pizzeria (1905), coal-fired oven, a few blocks east.
· Jack's Wife Freda (224 Lafayette) — brunch staple, lighter fare.
· Café Leon Dore (214 Mulberry) — coffee and a Mediterranean sandwich, attached to ALD.

🚶
Walk north on Broadway to 12th St. ~20 min, all on Broadway, technically out of SoHo and into the East Village / Union Square zone. Or grab the 6 train from Spring St to 14th St-Union Sq (~5 min).
Afternoon Forbidden Planet
832 Broadway at 12th St · comics, collectibles, games

One of the biggest comic and sci-fi shops in NYC. Comics (current issues, back issues, graphic novels), Funko Pops, anime figures, board games, Magic: The Gathering cards, Pokémon, Dungeons & Dragons.

Strand is 4 doors south. 828 Broadway, same side of the street.
Afternoon Strand Bookstore
828 Broadway at 12th St · since 1927

"18 miles of books." Three floors of new, used, and rare titles. The red awning, the dollar carts out front, the Strand tote bag — it's a NYC institution that has somehow survived every wave that swept the bookstores away.

📍Open in Maps
✨ Hidden gem

The Rare Book Room on the 3rd floor — first editions, signed copies, antiquarian finds. Often quieter than the main floors. Worth a peek even if you're not buying.

Track 2
5th Avenue Iconic

The classic NYC stretch. Central Park Zoo, Gapstow Bridge, the Plaza Hotel, Apple Cube, FAO Schwarz, and the flagship windows of 5th Avenue down to Rockefeller Center. A walking day, not a shopping day — though there's plenty of both.

🚶
Walk south through Central Park. From The Wallace, enter at 72nd & CPW, walk south along the east side ~25 min. Or grab the 1 to 59th-Columbus Circle and walk east through the park's southern end.
10:00 AM opening Central Park Zoo
East side of Central Park at 64th St · opens 10 AM

A small, walkable zoo — sea lions, snow leopards, grizzly bears, red pandas, penguins, and the Tropic Zone rainforest. Tickets at the gate.

📍Open in Maps
🎬 On screen

Madagascar (2005) — this is the zoo Alex the lion, Marty the zebra, Gloria the hippo, and Melman the giraffe escape from.

The Catcher in the Rye — Holden Caulfield visits with Phoebe.

🚶
Two minutes south to The Pond and Gapstow. Exit the Zoo and follow the path south along The Pond.
~late morning Gapstow Bridge
South end of The Pond · the Plaza Hotel in the background

One of the most photographed spots in Central Park. A small stone arch bridge over The Pond, with the Plaza Hotel and 5th Avenue skyline rising directly behind it.

📍Open in Maps
🎬 On screen

Home Alone 2: Lost in New York (1992) — the Pigeon Lady scenes were filmed here. Kevin feeds the pigeons by The Pond.

🚶
Exit the park at 59th & 5th. The Plaza is directly across the street.
~midday The Plaza Hotel
768 5th Ave at 59th · the lobby is open to the public

One of the most famous hotels in the world — the Plaza has been the backdrop for everything from The Great Gatsby to The Way We Were to nearly every NYC-set holiday movie. Walk in. The lobby and lower-level shops are open to non-guests.

📍Open in Maps
🎬 On screen

Home Alone 2: Lost in New York (1992) — Kevin checks into the Plaza with his dad's credit card. The Plaza has a permanent Home Alone gift shop in the lower level — props replicas, themed merch, the credit card line on a t-shirt.

Also: Crocodile Dundee, North by Northwest, Bride Wars, Scent of a Woman, and many others.

✨ Hidden gem

The Eloise shop — same lower level. The 1955 children's book "Eloise at the Plaza" is set here, and the shop sells the books, merch, and even has a mailbox where kids write letters to Eloise.

🚶
One block south on 5th. Apple Cube is at 5th & 58th, glass cube rising out of the sidewalk.
Any time Apple Fifth Avenue (The Cube)
767 5th Ave · 32-foot glass cube · store is underground

One of the most photographed Apple Stores in the world. The cube is the entrance; the store is below it.

📍Open in Maps
✨ Hidden gem

The store is open 24 hours, 7 days a week — one of very few in the world. If midday is crowded, you can come back any time, even at 3 AM.

🚶
Walk south on 5th. Tiffany's is at 57th, FAO Schwarz is at 49th in Rockefeller Plaza. About 10 blocks of flagship windows in between.
Afternoon Tiffany & Co. — The Landmark
727 5th Ave at 57th · the flagship since 1940

Recently renovated to its 10-floor glory. Walk in — the ground floor is open to anyone, and the Blue Box Café upstairs serves a proper Breakfast at Tiffany's tea (reservation only, usually weeks out, so don't plan on it day-of).

📍Open in Maps
🎬 On screen

Breakfast at Tiffany's (1961) — Holly Golightly's pre-dawn croissant window-gazing was filmed at the corner of 5th & 57th, right here.

🚶
Continue south on 5th. Saks at 50th, St. Patrick's Cathedral across from Saks, Rockefeller Plaza one block west.
Afternoon FAO Schwarz
30 Rockefeller Plaza · one block west of 5th Avenue

The toy store. Even if you're "too old" for a toy store, walk in. Magicians on the floor, a Jellycat diner, life-size stuffed animals, and the famous oversized floor piano.

📍Open in Maps
🎬 On screen

Big (1988) — Tom Hanks and Robert Loggia tap-dancing on the giant floor piano. The piano is still there, and yes — you can play it. Different store interior (the original FAO was at 5th & 58th, this location opened in 2018 in Rockefeller), but the floor piano lives on.

Wherever the day ends Saks & Rockefeller Plaza
Saks at 49th & 5th · Rockefeller Plaza one block west

Saks Fifth Avenue is the department store flagship — 10 floors, the windows are events of their own. Rockefeller Plaza is right next door if anyone wants to see it in daylight (different than from Top of the Rock at night).

St. Patrick's Cathedral sits directly across 5th from Saks, free to walk into, neo-Gothic spires, quiet inside.

🚇
Subway home from 50th St. B/D/F/M at Rockefeller Center, then transfer to A/C/E to 72nd. Or walk to the 1 train at 50th & Broadway, direct to 72nd. ~20 min total.
